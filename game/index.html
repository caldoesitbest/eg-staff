          Update 4✦Ascension

          What's new

          • Boss music. Every Sin has its own track: a heavy half-time crawl for Sloth, an overdriven gnashing stomp for Gluttony, a swung casino bounce for Greed, a harmonic-minor slow burn for Lust, a 174 bpm reese-bass rampage for Wrath, a tom-driven royal anthem for Pride, and Envy steals everyone else's melodies. Each chapter has its own theme too, and the music rises when the arena gets crowded and again when a boss is low.
          • A crosshair you can always see. Default "Auto" inverts whatever is behind it. Or pick a colour (or any colour), the size, the shape, an outline, and whether it turns red over a robot. Settings → Gameplay.
          • Seven chapters, seven looks. Each Sin tints the sky, the floor grid and the arena, with its own drifting dust, spores, gold glints, rose petals, embers, crystals or glitch shards.
          • Fewer stops. The Armory now opens after the 2nd and 4th wave of each chapter and after every boss (with a boss bounty: one Epic-or-better offer and a free reroll). Other waves: pick your Sin and go.
          • Fair bosses. Boss health no longer scales up to match your damage, so a strong build kills bosses faster, as it should. Later bosses are tougher to make up for it, and Pride's crown takes real work to break.
          • Know what hit you. A red arc points at whatever just hurt you, and the run summary shows what got you, your damage, biggest hit, top gun and stars spent.
          • Endless keeps flowing. Once every Sin and Augment is maxed, the card pick is skipped. Once the Armory has nothing left to sell you, it only opens when you need patching up.
          • Synergy hints. Cards and Armory offers tell you when they combo with what you already have.
          • Smoother on every device. The game picks a starting quality for your device and lowers the render resolution on its own before it drops effects, sprites stay sharp at any resolution, and less memory churn means fewer hitches.

          How to play

          • Climb the Sin Ladder: a sin boss every 5 waves, from @Sloth to @Envy on wave 35. Beat Envy to win, then keep climbing in endless.
          • Boost makes you untouchable for a moment. Boost through an attack at the last second for a Perfect: slow-mo, combo and a faster recharge.
          • Robots evolve (Mk II at wave 12, Mk III at 24). Overcharged elites carry a trait: cyan ring Shielded, white streaks Hasted, orange halo Volatile (bursts when it dies), green + Regenerating.
          • Robots telegraph: rippers show their charge path, gunners glow, boomers ring the blast, snipers paint a laser.
          • Grab stars. After every wave you pick a Sin or an Augment. After the 2nd and 4th wave of each chapter, and after every boss, the Armory opens: guns, gear, patch-ups. Lock offers you can't afford yet.
          • Charged shot: hold, then let go. Every gun charges into something different. Normal fire stops while you charge (until you get Capacitor).
          • Carry two guns and swap mid-fight: a crowd gun and a boss gun is a good start.
          • Stack Sins and gear that combo: cards and offers say when they synergise with what you have.
          • Co-op (2–4): one player hosts a lobby and shares the code. Everyone builds their own loadout and readies up between waves. Stand next to a downed teammate to revive them. The host should keep the game in front.
          • Quick kills build your combo. Getting hit drops it.
