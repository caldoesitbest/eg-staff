            Update 4.6✦Cosmic Shine

            What's new

            • A proper finish. Your score counts up at the end of a run, a new best gets stamped on, and the screen shows what you took home: Sigils, contracts, XP.
            • Your keys, your way. Settings has a Controls tab: rebind every key, and see the mouse, controller and touch layouts. Every hint in the game follows your keys. There's a HUD size slider too.
            • Pause shows your run: mode, difficulty, time, kills, best combo, and how you're doing against your best wave.
            • Moments that land. Combo milestones get called out, passing your best wave gets a cheer, and level-ups, boss portals, surges and mini-bosses each have their own sound.
            • Co-op fixes. Teammates see mini-bosses properly (a mini-boss could break a guest's screen), plus the boss portal, the mini-boss warning and red strike rings.
            • Fixes: the Challenges tab opens again, the wave banner lost its stray box and no longer sits on top of you, Armory labels stop running into the lock, and the HUD steps back while you pick cards or shop.

            Last update, 4.5 "Cosmic Legends":

            • Levels, 1 to 1000. Every run, challenge and achievement earns XP (more on harder difficulties). Each level pays Sigils, and seven levels unlock a hat you can't get any other way: Cadet Wings (10), Comet Antenna (25), Saturn Ring (50), Nebula Crown (100), Pulsar Helm (250), Supernova Corona (500) and The Thousandfold (1000).
            • Sigils mean something. Daily challenges and achievements pay big, firsts pay once, an ordinary run pays a little. Prices went up and your balance went up with them, so nothing you had lost value. Sigils now show under your stars.
            • Your stuff, everywhere. Items, Sigils and your level follow your account between the website, the Windows app and the Android app. Profile has a Restore button.
            • Friends and parties. A Friends tab, and two portals beside you to invite them. The leader picks the mode, everyone readies up, and it's co-op. A party comes back to the Hangar together.
            • It fights back. Hard and Nightmare robots flank and fire together, bosses surge as they weaken and arrive through a portal, three mini-bosses crash ordinary waves, and endless gets harder every single wave. Standing still now gets you killed.
            • Leaderboards by difficulty, plus Co-op and Boss Rush boards.
            • Flight school for new pilots (replay it from How to play), a new look for achievements, your build from anywhere, pick which gun a new one replaces, and much better controller support.
            • Discord: the Windows app shows what you're playing on your profile.

            Envious Glider✦v4.6

            Get the app

            The same game as this page, in its own full-screen app. Leaderboards, sign-in and co-op work whenever you're online.

            WindowsWindows 10 or 11 · 2.2 MBDownload .exe AndroidAndroid 7 or later · 0.9 MBDownload .apk
            • Windows: the app isn't code-signed yet, so Windows may say “Windows protected your PC”. Choose More info, then Run anyway. It uses Microsoft Edge WebView2, which Windows 10 and 11 already have.
            • Android: open the file when it finishes downloading. The first time, your phone asks you to allow installs from your browser. To update, install the new version over the old one: your progress stays. Sign in with Discord in the app (Google doesn't allow its sign-in inside apps).
            • iPhone and iPad: play right here. In Safari, tap Share, then Add to Home Screen for a full-screen icon.
            • The app keeps its own save, separate from this browser's.

            How to play

            • Climb the Sin Ladder: a sin boss every 5 waves, from @Sloth to @Envy on wave 35. Beat Envy to win, then keep climbing in endless.
            • Boost makes you untouchable for a moment. Boost through an attack at the last second for a Perfect: slow-mo, combo and a faster recharge.
            • Robots evolve (Mk II at wave 12, Mk III at 24; sooner on Hard and Nightmare). Overcharged elites carry a trait: cyan ring Shielded, white streaks Hasted, orange halo Shrapnel (bursts into shots when it dies), green + Regenerating.
            • Rarer mutants change how you fight them, and their name flashes when they land: Volatile bursts where it dies, Berserker snaps at half health, Armored has a weak spot behind, Shockwave sends out rings (boost through), Phantom shimmers then blinks to your side, Vampiric heals when it hits you, Replicator copies itself.
            • Robots telegraph: rippers show their charge path, gunners glow, boomers ring the blast, snipers paint a laser. They aim where you're heading: change direction once they commit.
            • Grab stars. After every wave you pick a Sin or an Augment. After the 2nd and 4th wave of each chapter, and after every boss, the Armory opens: guns, gear, patch-ups. Lock offers you can't afford yet.
            • Charged shot: hold, then let go. Every gun charges into something different. Normal fire stops while you charge (until you get Capacitor).
            • Carry two guns and swap mid-fight: a crowd gun and a boss gun is a good start.
            • Take a gun to Tier IV and beat the next sin boss: it evolves down one of two paths. Take two sins to level 3 and a Sin Fusion card can turn up.
            • Now and then something unusual happens: a Cosmic Cache to crack, a Corrupted Armory, a Sin Altar, an Elite Ambush.
            • Beat @Envy to open Boss Rush. Sin bosses, wins, Boss Rush, achievements and daily contracts earn Sigils: spend them in the Item Shop, and dress your astronaut in the Locker.
            • Stack Sins and gear that combo: cards and offers say when they synergise with what you have.
            • Co-op (2–4): one player hosts a lobby and shares the code. Everyone builds their own loadout and readies up between waves. Stand next to a downed teammate to revive them. The host should keep the game in front.
            • Quick kills build your combo. Getting hit drops it.
