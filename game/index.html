        Settings

        Update 3✦The Armory

        What's new

        • The Armory. A shop after every wave: seven guns (Scattergun, Pulse SMG, Rail Lance, Nova Launcher, Arc Caster, Heartseeker), 19 pieces of gear from Common to Legendary. Lock what you like, reroll the rest, buy a gun again to tier it up to IV.
        • Two gun slots. Swap with Q, the mouse wheel, 1/2, Y on a gamepad, or tap your gun on a phone.
        • Charged shots. Hold right-click (or E, LT, the Charge button) and let go. Every gun charges differently: a plasma orb, a pellet wall, a screen-wide rail beam, a cluster bomb, a storm orb, a heart swarm. New cards: Overcharge and Capacitor.
        • Lust, reworked. Shoot its kisses down. Its heart is exposed while it lures you, and boosting out of the lure stuns it. Heartbroken, it brings fakes: find the one with the beating heart.
        • Auto-aim on mouse and keyboard, plus a lighter Aim assist. T toggles it mid-run.
        • Graphics settings. Presets down to Potato, render resolution, frame-rate cap, particles, background, glow, trails, damage numbers, flashes, bold robot shots.
        • Feel. Hit markers, gun-specific sounds and recoil, a charge hum, a low-health heartbeat, clearer menus.

        How to play

        • Climb the Sin Ladder: a sin boss every 5 waves, from @Sloth to @Envy on wave 35. Beat Envy to win, then keep climbing in endless.
        • Boost makes you untouchable for a moment. Boost through an attack at the last second for a Perfect: slow-mo, combo and a faster recharge.
        • Robots evolve (Mk II at wave 12, Mk III at 24). Overcharged elites carry a trait: cyan ring Shielded, white streaks Hasted, orange halo Volatile (bursts when it dies), green + Regenerating.
        • Robots telegraph: rippers show their charge path, gunners glow, boomers ring the blast, snipers paint a laser.
        • Grab stars. After every wave you pick a card, then hit the Armory: guns, gear, patch-ups. Lock offers you can't afford yet.
        • Charged shot: hold, then let go. Every gun charges into something different. Normal fire stops while you charge (until you get Capacitor).
        • Carry two guns and swap mid-fight: a crowd gun and a boss gun is a good start.
        • Pick one of three sins after every wave. Stack them. Get weird.
        • Quick kills build your combo. Getting hit drops it.
