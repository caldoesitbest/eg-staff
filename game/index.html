        How to play

        • Climb the Sin Ladder: a sin boss every 5 waves, from @Sloth to @Envy on wave 35. Beat Envy to win, then keep climbing in endless.
        • Boost makes you untouchable for a moment. Boost through an attack at the last second for a Perfect: slow-mo, combo and a faster recharge.
        • Robots evolve (Mk II at wave 12, Mk III at 24). Overcharged elites carry a trait: cyan ring Shielded, white streaks Hasted, orange halo Volatile (bursts when it dies), green + Regenerating.
        • Robots telegraph: rippers show their charge path, gunners glow, boomers ring the blast, snipers paint a laser.
        • Grab stars. Between waves, spend them to reroll cards or patch up.
        • Pick one of three sins after every wave. Stack them. Get weird.
        • Quick kills build your combo. Getting hit drops it.
