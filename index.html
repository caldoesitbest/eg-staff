01 Why EG exists

Not another server thrown together.

Envious Gluttony™ started as a passion project between a few friends who wanted somewhere people actually stay: to talk, laugh, argue about nothing, meet people and have a genuinely good time.

Nobody here is a spectator. The ideas come from the chat, the features come from the suggestions forum, and the culture comes from whoever shows up.

All rise. There's a new posse making waves in the scene.
